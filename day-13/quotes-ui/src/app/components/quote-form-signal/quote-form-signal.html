This is an experimental version of the Create Quote form, built to try out a newer way of handling forms in Angular. It works the same way, with two small differences:

  • Author and Text stop you from typing past their character limit as you go, instead of only telling you after you submit.
  • If you submit without filling them in, it won't automatically jump your cursor to the first empty field.
